# Report Specification

## 1 Executive Overview
KPI card: Net Sales, Gross Margin, GM%, Budget Variance. Line chart mensile, matrice regione/store, waterfall scostamento budget.

## 2 Sales Analysis
Trend, categoria/prodotto, canale, top/bottom store, drill-through documento.

## 3 Customer Analysis
Active/New Customers, segmento, regione, consenso, frequenza acquisto.

## 4 Inventory
Stock, available, days of cover, slow movers, warehouse vs store.

## 5 Data Quality & Operations
Ultimo refresh, SLA, righe scartate, issue per regola e trend.
